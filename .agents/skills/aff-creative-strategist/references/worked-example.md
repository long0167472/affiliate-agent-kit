# Worked example: one product, one hook test

This is an **illustration**, not a recommendation to promote this product. All shots below require an actual sample and original footage. Do not claim the product succeeds until the filmed test shows it.

## ProductBrief extract

Product: one exact adhesive cable holder SKU, black, 6-pack. Listing says it organizes loose desk cables. The producer has a real sample and permission to use their own phone footage. No evidence yet about adhesive lifetime, surface compatibility, maximum cable weight, or current price. Safe claim: “I used it to keep this charging cable within reach on this desk during this filmed demonstration.” Unsafe claims: “never falls,” “works on every surface,” “best on Shopee.”

## CreativePlan: 20 seconds

Viewer moment: charging cable repeatedly slips behind a desk. Mechanism: visible micro-demonstration with a practical payoff. A second concept could start from the annoyance; a third could compare desk with/without holder. Select the micro-demonstration because the first frame contains an observable action.

| Time | Picture / frame | Audio and on-screen text | Purpose and proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–0.7 s | Tight shot: hand releases charging cable beside desk; cable begins to fall | Natural cable tap; text “Lại rơi sau bàn?” | Product-relevant problem immediately visible |
| 0.7–2.5 s | Cable drops; hand retrieves it | Voice: “Mỗi lần sạc là mình phải cúi nhặt.” | Specific friction; if creator never experienced this, use neutral narration instead of first person |
| 2.5–5 s | Close-up of **actual SKU** attached to clean desk edge | Text: “Thử kẹp dây này” | Reveal exact product; attachment method visible |
| 5–10 s | One uninterrupted shot placing cable in holder, releasing it, lifting and replacing it | Natural sound; sparse labels | Real demonstration, no cut hiding a failure |
| 10–15 s | Wider desk shot: cable stays accessible; show package or quantity accurately | Voice names one observed benefit and one limitation if seen | Payoff and context |
| 15–20 s | Hand repeats retrieval once; product remains visible | Text/voice: “Xem đúng mẫu trong giỏ hàng” for Shopee, or target platform's native CTA | Clear next action; no price claim unless refreshed |

## Claim-to-shot map

“Cable stays within reach in this demonstration” → uninterrupted shot 5–10 s and wider shot 10–15 s. “Adhesive lasts for weeks” → **no shot; do not say it**. “Six holders included” → film and count all six only if that exact delivered variant was checked.

## Hook test

Variant A uses the 0–2.5 s falling-cable opening above. Variant B begins 0–2.5 s with a close shot of one-hand cable retrieval from the holder and text “Không cần mò dây sau bàn”; both then use the **same 2.5–20 s body, caption style, product, CTA and roughly comparable posting slots**. Hypothesis: A's visible frustration earns more early holds than B's result-first opening. Disconfirming outcome: B has equal or better early hold across repeated comparable blocks. Measure the account's actually available early-retention metric; if unavailable, mark it `UNAVAILABLE` and use the nearest documented platform measure with its numerator/denominator. Track product clicks and confirmed commission separately.

## Cross-review questions

- Would a stranger understand the problem before the voice starts?
- Does the 5–10 s shot genuinely prove what the text promises?
- Is the cable holder still the subject throughout the Shopee version?
- If the clip looks smooth but no one clicks, is the issue hook, product relevance, offer, or CTA? What next test separates them?
