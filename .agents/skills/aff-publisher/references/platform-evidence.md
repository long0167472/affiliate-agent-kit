# Platform evidence and live checks

This is a dated research baseline (23 October 2026), not a permanent policy mirror. Publisher and product role must reopen the relevant official page when applying a rule to a live account.

## TikTok Shop Vietnam

- [Creator Product Marketplace](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837827107342081&lang=en): creators can search products/categories, inspect ratings, price, commission and sample opportunities; recommendations are personalized to product-interest history. Use it as a candidate source, not proof of sales potential.
- [Creator affiliate eligibility](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10016840&lang=en): the Vietnam policy distinguishes full Affiliate Account (normally at least 1,000 followers and other eligibility conditions) from Link-Only Account (no follower minimum, external link sharing but no in-app short-video/LIVE commission). [Creator Adjustment Program](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?identity=1&knowledge_id=2356948167771905&role=1), dated 12 October 2026, offers eligible creators with 200–999 followers early access: at most seven shoppable videos per week, products only from shops rated at least four, and no campaign access. Recheck the account's real permission state before switching accounts.
- [How TikTok recommends content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content): browsing interactions personalize the user's For You feed. This does not establish that an account used for browsing receives lower distribution for its own uploads; account choice should rely on actual creator status, audience fit and measured posts.
- [AIGC policy](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6832782790018833&lang=en): AI product visuals must match real color, design, function and listing. No fabricated effects or stories; use required AIGC label.
- [Unoriginal content](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837791128880898&lang=en): copied clips, repeated scripts and superficial edits may be restricted; original perspective and genuine product demonstration matter.
- [Content Posting API](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post): direct video post requires `video.publish`, user authorization, and an audited client for public visibility; metadata includes commercial and AI toggles. The documented request schema reviewed here does not establish a Shop product-anchor field. Check current access before promising automated shoppable publication.
- [AI Video Maker](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2533343863097105&lang=en) is documented in Seller Center. Do not assume creator affiliate access.

## Shopee Video Vietnam

- [Shopee Affiliate dashboard](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/152867-H%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Bng-d%E1%BA%ABn-s%E1%BB%AD-d%E1%BB%A5ng-h%E1%BB%87-th%E1%BB%91ng-Shopee-Affiliate): KOL/KOC account surfaces include product commission lists, Xtra offers, campaign invitations, clicks, order states and payments. Use its product and commission sections as candidate sources when the authorized account exposes them.
- [Shopee affiliate anti-fraud policy](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/206670): restricts robot/automated search and automated collection of marketplace product and price data. Automated Shopee discovery requires an expressly authorized data route; otherwise analyze permitted exports or use user-assisted account browsing.
- [Commission lookup](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/123037): rates vary by time, category, channel and account; inspect the current app/dashboard value for the exact product before ranking candidates.
- [Affiliate with Shopee Video](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/141537): official flow attaches products in the app. Shopee recommends clear vertical video, relevant linked products, real people/body parts, and visible product use. It warns against third-party watermarks.
- [Video quality rules](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/177021): the actual product should be the video's subject; link should match the product shown. Demonstration is preferable when feasible.
- [AI content and labels](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/202687-%5BC%E1%BA%ACP-NH%E1%BA%ACT%5D-Nh%E1%BB%AFng-%C4%91i%E1%BB%81u-c%E1%BA%A7n-bi%E1%BA%BFt-v%E1%BB%81-n%E1%BB%99i-dung-AI-v%C3%A0-nh%C3%A3n-d%C3%A1n-AI-tr%C3%AAn-Shopee-Video): Shopee describes AI-created or AI-edited video, in whole or in part, and instructs creators to use its AI label; the account owner remains responsible. The precise boundary for trivial edits is unclear, so inspect current examples and label whenever the content includes generated or materially AI-altered image, voice, or video rather than guessing that a small edit is exempt.
- [Posting and enforcement rules](https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/185893-Quy-%C4%91%E1%BB%8Bnh-%C4%91%C4%83ng-Shopee-Video-v%C3%A0-c%C3%A1c-h%C3%ACnh-th%E1%BB%A9c-x%E1%BB%AD-l%C3%BD-vi-ph%E1%BA%A1m): warns against static images for much of the video, repetitive zooming, misleading curiosity, and unlabeled AI content.

For Shopee creator posting, the official help reviewed describes the in-app flow. No verified public affiliate video publishing API was found in this research; this is an **unverified capability**, not a claim that no API exists. Confirm partner/account access before attempting automation. Do not use unofficial private endpoints or session-cookie automation as a default publishing architecture.
